# Research report template

## Answer

Give the supported answer to the question. Cite the direct source next to each
material factual claim. Mark inferences and proposals explicitly.

## Evidence and scope

For each material source, record its title, publisher, direct URL, relevant
version or publication date, retrieval date and the claim it supports. State
which question, date range and environments were examined.

## Conflicts and uncertainty

Explain conflicting sources, assumptions, unavailable material and limits of
the evidence. A missing source is not evidence that a capability is absent.

## Next evidence

Describe the smallest additional observation or authoritative source needed to
resolve each material gap. Do not claim a proposed experiment was executed.
